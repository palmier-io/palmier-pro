## Shared design and timing principles

The map remains a continuous spatial surface. Geographic objects, fills, routes and attached labels use one projection and camera transform. A wide view answers “where”; a close view answers the current detail. Camera moves settle before a dense fact, while color identity survives the move. Relief supplies context; colored fills, bright strokes/glow and short bold outlined text provide the information hierarchy.

Two text layers have different timing. Headings/questions/numbers are concise visual assertions held long enough to read. Spoken phrases are smaller transcript-aligned captions; they should not cover the same geographic target or platform controls. The supplied references vary their quantity/card/callout use, so a single universal subtitle/transition preset would lose the differences.

Narration is continuous and assertive, with names/numbers emphasized and short pauses around reveals or twists. A strong performance depends on voice choice, pronunciation, script cadence and dialogue-first mixing. Measured speech timing governs the scene frames; illustrative storyboard timings are not alignment evidence. The app reuses local neural/system speech, pinned narration intent, transcription/captions and measured audio finishing. It does not certify the reference narrator's identity or provide a cloned voice.

## Added abilities and remaining creative limits

The native renderer now supports the underlying map grammar: real geographic outlines, relief/custom backgrounds, region components and timed color/image fills, camera easing/pan/zoom/rotation/planar tilt, revealed curved/via routes, approximate distance labels, moving ships/packages/vehicles/people/money/industry symbols, custom image objects and deterministic hazard/weather effects, plus timed bold typography. Three offline skills distinguish story structure, object choreography and narration/audio synchronization.

The map output is a normal silent library video, with a saved specification and undo/redo. Existing graphic, caption, audio and export tools supply the other tracks. This is an equirectangular renderer, not a cinematic 3D globe or photorealistic battle/shipping simulator. Exact aesthetic similarity and intelligible narration still require playback of the produced film. Use original scripts and verified topic data, and inspect every generated visual before final delivery.
